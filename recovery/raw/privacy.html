Introduction
SAFER Utah is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal information we collect, how we collect and use that information, and your rights regarding your personal data. By using our website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.
 
Information We Collect
Personal Information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, postal address, phone number, and other contact details.

  • Voluntary Information: Information you provide voluntarily through forms, surveys, petitions, or sign-ups for newsletters and events.

Non-Personal Information:

  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and other technical information.

  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our website, such as pages visited, links clicked, and other interaction data.

SMS Opt-In

  • Consent and Data will not be shared with third parties.

​
How We Collect Information
We collect information directly from you when you:

  • Fill out forms or surveys.

  • Sign up for newsletters, events, or other communications.

  • Donate.

  • Contact us through our website or other communication channels.

We also collect information indirectly through the following:

  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies.

  • Analytics tools that monitor usage and interaction data.

​
How We Use and Share Information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Communication: Send updates, newsletters, and other information about SAFER Utah's activities.

  • Engagement: To inform you about events, opportunities for involvement, and ways to support.

  • Donations: Process and manage your donations, including sending receipts and acknowledgments.

  • Improvement: To analyze and improve our website, communication strategies, and outreach efforts.

  • Legal Obligations: To comply with applicable laws and regulations, including campaign finance laws.

​
We share your information only to fulfill our obligations to you, and we do not share or sell your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist with our website operations, payment processing, and other services.

  • Legal Authorities: If required by law or in response to legal processes.

​
Protection of Your Information
We implement various security measures to protect your personal information, including encryption, secure servers, and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices
You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can request a copy of our personal information about you.

  • Correction: You can request that we correct any inaccuracies in your personal information.

  • Deletion: You can request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal and contractual obligations.

  • Opt-Out: If you are receiving text messages or emails from us and wish to stop receiving them, respond with either “STOP” or click the “UNSUBSCRIBE” button at the bottom of the email from which you received the message. Once we receive your opt-out request, you will no longer receive further text messages from us.

​
Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
