On the Rocks: Gaps in Utah's Alcohol Policy Landscape

Voiceless Consumers: Utah's responsible drinkers deserve a seat at the table. Currently, those most affected by alcohol regulations are excluded from crucial negotiations.

 

SAFER: Utah amplifies your voice, ensuring that sensible, liberty-minded policies reflect the needs of everyday Utahns.

Polarized Positions: Extreme viewpoints and conflicting interests hinder progress in Utah's alcohol policy landscape.

 

SAFER: Utah bridges the gap, advocating for pragmatic solutions that balance individual freedom with community well-being, free from ideological or financial bias.

Bad Data: Informed decisions require accurate information. Utah's alcohol policies often rely on skewed statistics and biased data.

 

SAFER: Utah champions transparency and pushes for unbiased research to guide sensible, fact-based regulations that respect personal liberty."

So... What's Next?

We launched SAFER to give you a voice. In just hours, we received more than 300 survey responses and thousands of emails, texts, and visits to our website. In February, SAFER was just an idea to further safe and sensible alcohol laws. Now, we’re a coalition with crafted plans and priorities built by input from consumers, lawmakers, industry, and public safety experts. We’ve even retained lobbyists and grassroots organizers to bring the consumer’s voice to the table of policy negotiations. Before anything is finalized, we want to hear more from you. In the coming weeks, watch for survey results, strategic updates, and more across our social platforms and keep checking back her for updates!

Who We Are

We’re Utahns committed to reasonable alcohol laws and safe communities.

 

As consumers, SAFER UTAH bridges business needs and prevention efforts to reach real-world solutions. 

What We Do

We lobby for data-driven liquor laws without sacrificing safety or liberty.

 

We aim to replace overregulation with commonsense, enforceable, effective policies that protect responsible fun and curb alcohol abuse. We give back safety education and data to track our progress.

How We Do It

Through extenstive, community-focused research.

​

Our team of experts and consumers distill Utah voices into informed proposals that serve everyone, not just special interests. Our better beehive celebrates safe drinkers, because abuse hurts families and businesses alike.
