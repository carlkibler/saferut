What we work on

Utah deserves alcohol policy built on evidence, not reflex.

01

Consumer representation

Responsible drinkers are affected by alcohol regulation but too rarely included when policy is negotiated. We make room for their experience.

02

Practical common ground

We look past polarized positions for enforceable ideas that support responsible choice, local businesses, and community safety.

03

Better evidence

Good laws need trustworthy measures. We advocate for transparent data, clear outcomes, and policies that can be tested against reality.

Who we are

A better beehive starts with a better conversation.

SAFER Utah is a coalition of Utahns working toward reasonable alcohol laws and safer communities. We connect the people who consume alcohol responsibly with the people who regulate it, sell it, and work to prevent harm.

Our aim is simple: replace ineffective overregulation with clear, workable policy. Personal freedom and public safety are not opposing teams.

What guides us

Responsible choice. Honest data. Laws that work.

  • Personal responsibility and safer drinking habits
  • The freedom to drink responsibly—or not drink at all
  • Data-informed, measurable approaches to regulation
  • Room for local businesses and entrepreneurship
  • Simple rules that people can understand and follow
