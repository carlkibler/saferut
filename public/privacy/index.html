Privacy

Plainly: we only want the information you mean to give us.

This site does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or cookies. When you email us, take an external survey, donate, or use a social-media link, that provider handles information under its own privacy terms.

We use information you send us to respond, communicate about SAFER Utah's work, operate advocacy and educational activities, process donations through our provider, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information we hold by emailing info@saferut.org. Some records may need to be retained for legal or financial reporting.
